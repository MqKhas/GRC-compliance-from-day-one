# Phase 1: Risk Assessment

What could go wrong, how bad it would be, and what I'm doing about it. Every control in later phases traces back to a risk here.

| Artifact | What it answers |
|---|---|
| [methodology.md](methodology.md) | How risks are scored and decided: NIST SP 800-30 scales, the risk matrix, risk appetite and acceptance criteria (also meets ISO 27001 6.1.2) |
| [asset-threat-register.md](asset-threat-register.md) | What's being protected, who or what could cause harm, and the weaknesses that make harm possible |
| [risk-register.md](risk-register.md) | The short version: heat map, top risks, every risk's level and treatment, ATT&CK techniques |
| [risk-register.csv](risk-register.csv) / [.xlsx](risk-register.xlsx) | The full register: threat events, controls, scoring reasons, inherent, current and target risk |
| [risk-treatment-plan.md](risk-treatment-plan.md) | Actions, Annex A controls, due dates, accepted risks and the risk owner's sign-off (ISO 27001 6.1.3) |

Methodology and treatment decisions: D-010 to D-016 in [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md). Primer: [learning/primer-phase-1-risk.md](../learning/primer-phase-1-risk.md).
