| D-009 | 2026-10-31 | Build one common control framework. SOC 2 Type I readiness is the primary target and ISO 27001:2022 readiness the second. | D-010 | 2026-10-31 | Risk scales: five levels (Very Low to Very High) for likelihood and impact, per NIST SP 800-30. One likelihood test works for both attacks and accidents and fits a yearly review. Table I-2 keeps rare but severe risks visible | M. Khas |
| D-011 | 2026-10-31 | Impact rated by the worst effect on people, the service or the operator, using the scale in the methodology.
